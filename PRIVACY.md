## Information processed and purposes

The app stores Atlassian account identifiers for document registrants and assigned reviewers; page identifiers; review intervals and dates; pause and comment notification preferences; delivery outcomes and timestamps; and privacy reporting and retry timestamps. These records support scheduling, access checks, duplicate prevention, troubleshooting and privacy requests.

The interface retrieves current page titles and uses Confluence permissions. It does not store copies of page bodies or user email addresses in its application database. When a user enables comment reminders, the app posts a Confluence comment mentioning the assigned reviewer. Confluence permissions and notification settings govern who can see the comment and receive associated notifications.

The app sends account identifiers and data-age information to Atlassian's personal data reporting service to check for account erasure or update requests. It has no advertising, third-party analytics or external AI integration and does not sell app personal data. It makes no automated decisions concerning employment, credit or similar eligibility.

## Hosting, recipients and international processing

App records are stored in Atlassian Forge. In-scope residency data includes the review settings, account and page identifiers, notification records and privacy metadata stored there. Storage follows the host Confluence location under Atlassian's supported residency settings. Supported locations include Australia, Canada, Germany, Ireland (EU), India, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the United States; a Global location is managed by Atlassian. Runtime processing can occur outside the storage location. A Korean provider address does not imply Korean-only data storage.

See [Forge residency details](https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/forge/data-residency/) and [Atlassian subprocessors](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/sub-processors). Administrative support may involve access by the provider in Korea. The provider will apply any additional safeguards required for its international transfers. No blanket claim of compliance for every transfer or jurisdiction is made.

If you choose to email support, your address, message and attachments are processed through Google Gmail and may be accessed by the provider in Korea. Email is separate from Forge residency and is not needed for routine app operation. Google handles its infrastructure under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Do not email page bodies, credentials or unnecessary personal information. Information may also be disclosed where legally required, with appropriate limitations.

## Retention and deletion

Review settings remain until removed. A daily housekeeping job removes inactive delivery records older than 365 days. Active review-cycle duplicate prevention records and test records for registered pages remain while needed to prevent duplicate comments. Privacy tracking metadata is removed when no associated app records remain.

Users can delete their own app records and remove their reviewer assignments through the app. Atlassian account erasure or update responses also trigger removal of associated app records and assignments. Outages or API limits can delay scheduled processing. Deletion does not remove original Confluence pages or comments already posted; the customer manages these in Confluence.

Deleted records cease to be accessible to the app. Atlassian may retain recovery copies and backups under its platform lifecycle, including after uninstall. See [Forge data lifecycle](https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/forge/storage-reference/hosted-storage-data-lifecycle/). Immediate deletion of every platform backup is not promised.

Support correspondence will be deleted within 90 days after the request is closed unless retention is required by law or to address a specific unresolved dispute. Records subject to a separate legal retention obligation will be restricted to that purpose and kept only as required. This is an operational commitment of the provider, not an automated app feature.

## Choices and requests

Comment notifications are off by default; users can pause or remove entries. Diagnostic copying omits page titles, bodies, page IDs, account IDs and email addresses, and does not automatically transmit the report. You choose whether to send it.

Contact the provider to request access, correction, a copy, deletion or restrictions, or to object to processing where applicable. We will verify identity and authority and coordinate customer-controlled requests with the relevant administrator. You may also contact the competent privacy supervisory authority. Providing the app's minimum account and page identifiers is necessary to operate its review workflow; support email is optional.
