# Page Review Reminder — Customer Data Processing Addendum

Version: 13 October 2026, revision 2. California customer provisions added with provider approval.

Provider: dahyun kim, Republic of Korea. Contact: danielanderic@gmail.com.

## Scope and instructions

This addendum applies where the provider processes personal data for a customer using Page Review Reminder. The customer determines the purposes of its document review workflow and acts as controller, or as a processor authorized by its controller. The provider acts as processor or subprocessor for that workflow. The provider acts independently for its own support correspondence and legal administration, as described in the Privacy Notice.

The provider will process customer personal data only to supply, secure and support the app, follow the customer's documented instructions, or meet a legal requirement. The order, configured app actions and this addendum constitute instructions. The provider will inform the customer if an instruction appears to violate applicable data protection law, and about legally required processing where permitted.

## Processing description

Data subjects are customer users who register documents or are assigned as reviewers. Stored data consists of Atlassian account IDs, page IDs, review settings and dates, notification and pause preferences, delivery records, and privacy-reporting timestamps. The app also retrieves current page titles and checks permissions. It does not store page body copies or user email addresses in its application database. Processing includes storage, retrieval, scheduling, permission checks, posting opted-in Confluence comments, troubleshooting and deletion.

Processing lasts while records are retained for the service. The customer should not put sensitive personal information in support requests or use this app to process data outside its documented functionality.

## Security and people

The provider will maintain measures appropriate to the processing, including restricting administrative access, authenticating app requests, enforcing page and record access checks, minimizing diagnostic data, maintaining dependencies and investigating security defects. People authorized by the provider to access customer data must be bound by confidentiality obligations. Hosting and platform protections are supplied by Atlassian; no independent security certification is asserted for this app.

## Subprocessing and locations

The customer authorizes Atlassian to host and operate the app through Forge, including its storage, APIs and platform operations, with the subprocessors Atlassian discloses in its [subprocessor list](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/sub-processors). Forge storage location follows the host Confluence location and supported Atlassian residency settings. Processing and authorized support access may take place outside the customer's country, including access by the provider in the Republic of Korea.

The provider remains responsible for its subprocessing arrangements as required by applicable law. Before appointing an additional app subprocessor, the provider will give at least 30 days' notice through the published subprocessor information and an available customer contact. Customers may raise a substantiated data protection objection; the parties will seek a solution, and if none is feasible the customer may discontinue the affected service under its agreement. International transfers requiring additional safeguards must have a lawful transfer mechanism in place before they occur. This addendum alone is not a substitute for any required standard contractual clauses or transfer assessment.

## Assistance and incidents

The provider will reasonably assist the customer with verified individual rights requests, security inquiries, impact assessments and regulator consultation, taking account of the nature of processing and information available. Requests relating to customer-controlled data may be referred to the customer's administrator. The provider will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the customer's app data, provide available information needed for the customer's response, and cooperate in containment and remediation. Notification does not itself admit liability.

## Return, deletion and verification

The customer can request return or deletion of its app data through the contact above while the app remains accessible. The provider will verify authority and coordinate the request. Deletion removes records from the app's accessible primary storage; Atlassian's recovery and backup lifecycle may retain copies temporarily. Source pages and comments remain within the customer's Confluence controls. Any retention legally required must be limited to that purpose and protected.

The provider will make relevant compliance information available and permit reasonable, confidential verification of its obligations. Verification must respect other customers' data and platform security; access to Atlassian infrastructure is governed by Atlassian's own assurance processes. The customer and provider will coordinate scope and timing without limiting mandatory regulator powers or legal rights.

## California customer information

Where CCPA applies to customer personal information, the provider acts as the customer's service provider or contractor. The specified purposes are storing review settings, checking page permissions, recording reviews, sending opted-in comments, and supporting those functions.

The provider will not sell or share that information, use it for unrelated commercial purposes, or process it outside the customer relationship, except as CCPA permits. Combining it with other customers' information or independently collected information is prohibited except as permitted by CCPA.

The provider will meet applicable CCPA requirements, maintain required privacy protections and reasonable security, assist with customer-directed consumer requests and applicable assessments, and notify the customer if it determines it cannot meet these obligations. The customer will supply necessary request information and instructions.

The customer may take reasonable steps to verify compliance and, upon notice, stop and remedy unauthorized processing. Applicable verification and confidentiality provisions in this DPA do not limit mandatory rights. Any subcontracted processing must be governed by a contract meeting applicable CCPA requirements.

This addendum prevails over inconsistent customer agreement terms concerning the processing it covers. It does not waive mandatory rights or establish compliance with every jurisdiction by itself.
