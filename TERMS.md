## Service and support

Page Review Reminder is a Confluence Cloud app for tracking page review dates and assigned reviewers. Each registering user can maintain up to 100 review entries. Optional reminder comments must be enabled per entry. Scheduled checks run daily; processing time depends on Atlassian availability and limits. Confluence controls notification delivery. The service does not independently send reminder emails or verify that a document's contents are correct.

Support is provided through the contact above. No separate uptime SLA, guaranteed response deadline, or 24-hour support is offered. This does not remove obligations in the standard agreement or applicable mandatory law. Security and privacy requests should identify the affected site without including passwords or API tokens.

## Data and customer controls

Review entries can be paused, marked reviewed or removed. Users can delete their own app records and remove their reviewer assignments using the app. Page content and previously posted Confluence comments are controlled in Confluence and are not deleted by that action. Customers may request a copy of their app records through support; identity and authority must be verified before disclosure. The app has no bulk export interface currently.

The provider does not sell app personal data, use it for advertising, or send it to external AI services. Support messages voluntarily sent by email are handled separately as described in the Privacy Notice.
